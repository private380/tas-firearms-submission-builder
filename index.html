Before you begin

This is independent software. It is not operated by, endorsed by or connected with any government agency.

  1. Complete all 21 amendment questions.
  2. Review and generate your submission.
  3. Select Save submission as PDF and save the PDF to your device.

For PDF saving on iPhone, open this website in Safari rather than inside Facebook Messenger.

This builder is designed primarily for lawful firearms owners, primary producers, sporting shooters, clubs and dealers. It uses the same amendment descriptions and lawful-owner focus as the Guide.

Anonymous community results When you save a PDF, this builder records only anonymous totals for the positions and suggested options selected. It does not send your name, email address, free-text comments or PDF to Tas Policy Watch.
